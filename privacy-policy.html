Privacy Policy — Private Offline AI Companions

Last updated: September 2026

1. The short version

This app collects nothing, transmits nothing and needs no account. Your messages, characters, favorites, name and the AI model itself live only in this phone's local storage. We have no servers, so we never receive your data at all.

2. Data we do NOT collect

No names, emails, identifiers, locations, contacts, analytics events, crash reports tied to you, or advertising data. There is simply no pipeline for any of it.

3. When the internet is used

Exactly two cases: (a) downloading an AI model file when you explicitly tap Download, and (b) loading this app's updates from the store. Chatting itself never uses the network — airplane mode is the proof. Downloaded files are verified on-device before use.

4. Storage on your phone

The app stores your chats and the downloaded AI model in its private local storage (about 1.5 GB with a model). Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Deleting a chat, a model or the whole app removes the corresponding data permanently.

5. Children

This app is strictly 18+, is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone of any age.

6. Contact

Privacy questions: privateofflineaicompanion@gmail.com. If you report offensive AI content, send only a description — never include private personal data.

7. Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version ships with the app update.
